System Latency Meter

What this tool does and how to use it

This tool helps you manually measure the time from a mouse click 🖱️ to the beep 🔊 produced when the system registers that click. It records both sounds through your microphone. On the waveform, place M at the start of the Mouse click 🖱️ sound and B at the start of the Beep 🔊 sound; the result shows the elapsed time, adjusted for any beep 🔊 delay you set.

  1. Take your headset off.
  2. Place your headset's microphone as close as possible to the mouse 🖱️ button.
  3. Disable noise cancellation and similar audio processing in your microphone driver or software (for example, SteelSeries GG) so the microphone picks up the mouse click 🖱️ sounds clearly.
  4. Turn up your headset volume enough for the microphone to clearly pick up the beep 🔊.
  5. Press Start recording and keep quiet.
  6. Click the large test area to trigger the beep 🔊. You can click more than once if you want to record several attempts.
  7. Press Stop recording to display the sound graph.
  8. Use the mouse wheel over the graph to zoom in or out. Move the yellow M bar to the beginning of the Mouse click 🖱️ sound, and the magenta B bar to the beginning of the Beep 🔊 sound. The measured latency appears below the graph.
Advanced: If the beep 🔊 is too short or long, adjust its duration in Beep 🔊 options.
Advanced: If the click sound is too close to the beep 🔊, add a delay in Beep 🔊 options. The tool subtracts this added delay from the displayed measurement.
Advanced: If the waveform is noisy, increase Zero sound below (% of peak) in Sound graph options to remove low-level microphone noise.
